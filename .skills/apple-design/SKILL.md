# Apple Design
Distilled from Apple's design language:
- Translucent materials (`backdrop-blur-2xl bg-white/[0.04]` / `bg-[#161618]/70`, `border-white/[0.08]`)
- Tactile `:active` button response (`active:scale-[0.98] transition-all duration-150`)
- Apple SF Pro typographic hierarchy (`-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "SF Pro Text", "SF Pro Display"`)
- Floating pill segmented controls
- Clean, physical micro-interactions and refined shadows
